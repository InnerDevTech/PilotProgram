Expected outcome

By the end of Sprint 3 the camera above the desk and the projector should share one coordinate system. Running calibrate.py finds all 24 dots and produces a mapping with under 2% average error, checked against calibration_check.jpg rather than taken on trust.

With that in place, a printed marker on a real object should make a ring appear around that object on the desk — blue with its name if it is listed in objects.json, amber if it is not — and the ring should stay on the object as it is slid around, within about 2 cm of its centre.

This is the last sprint of the MVP. If Sprints 1 to 3 pass, the project is a success even if Sprint 4 never happens.

Demo checklist

  • The calibration page shows 24 dots and the bridge says Calibrated.
  • Average calibration error is under 2%.
  • A registered object shows its name and a blue ring.
  • Moving the object moves the ring with it.
  • An unregistered object shows an amber ring.

Definition of done

The ring stays on the object for 2 minutes of moving it around.

Risk to watch: the projector or camera gets bumped. Mark their positions with tape, and re-run calibration after any move.
