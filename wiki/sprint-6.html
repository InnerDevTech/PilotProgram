Expected outcome

By the end of Sprint 6 the project should be presented, not just built. A 5-minute talk covering the problem, how it works, a live demo, what went wrong and what is next; a one-page handout with the wiring and loop diagrams; and a rehearsal in front of a teacher or friend with the feedback written down and acted on.

Everything should be backed up to a USB drive — project folder and calibration file both — before showcase day, and the Sprint 4 recording kept to hand in case the live demo misbehaves.

The project is finished when the whole-project done definition passes and the reflection is written.
